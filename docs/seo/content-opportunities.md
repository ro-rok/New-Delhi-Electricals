## Content rules

- Do not publish “full form of MCB” content just because it has volume; it has weak enquiry value.
- Avoid unsafe DIY installation instructions. Selection/explanation content should tell readers to use a qualified electrician for installation/testing.
- “Best” comparisons must use objective selection criteria and current catalogue facts; do not fabricate brand superiority.
- Every guide needs one primary commercial parent and contextual product/brand links.
- Do not create a separate article for every near-identical question variation.
- Price guides should be evergreen and quote/range driven unless the site can reliably maintain current list prices.
