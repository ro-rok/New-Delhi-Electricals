## SERP observations

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Pattern and page inspection benchmark

| Competitor pattern | Why it works | NDE response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific brand + category claims | Matches buyer’s brand-led journey | Add only where NDE’s stocked collection has depth and claim is supportable. |
| Location, address, hours, phone and WhatsApp | Helps local-commercial trust and conversion | Keep first-party NAP/GBP data consistent, with a real contact/store page. |
| Contractor/project language | Captures high-value procurement queries | Add a project/bulk requirement CTA to wires and circuit protection categories. |
| Product-range navigation | Resolves selection friction | Link category → brand → exact models and cross-link compatible plates/boxes/switches. |
| Brand badges / authorisation | Trust signal, but high compliance risk | Use only evidence-backed badges/wording; retain supporting manufacturer documentation. |

## Realistic weak points to outperform

- Directory results have little first-party product depth or compatibility guidance.
- Many local seller pages show broad brand lists but weak model-level navigation.
- Manufacturer pages often do not resolve local stock, quantity, quote or multi-brand selection.
- Generic content frequently makes absolute technical claims; NDE can be more useful by using manufacturer/BIS references, a review date and a qualified-electrician safety boundary.

## Competitor-monitoring cadence

Monthly, capture the first page for the four commercial clusters above plus proven GSC queries. Record domain, URL, title/H1, result type, local-pack presence, CTA, location proof and any new dedicated page. Do not infer traffic, ranking history, backlinks or keyword overlap without a source export.
