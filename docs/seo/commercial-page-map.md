Because no GSC performance export exists, rows receive **0 of 20 for GSC evidence**, not an invented score.

## Pages deliberately deferred or rejected

- City × brand × category URLs for Noida, Gurugram/Gurgaon, Ghaziabad or Faridabad: no evidence yet of unique service content or distinct intent; doorway risk is high.
- Separate `Havells switches Delhi`, `Anchor switches Delhi`, `Lauritz Knudsen switches Delhi`: first satisfy demand through improved brand and category pages; avoid brand/category duplication.
- `MCB price Delhi`, `RCCB price Delhi`, `modular switches price Delhi`: do not create price pages unless live pricing and a distinct comparison/price-list intent can be maintained.
- Filters, colour, pack-size, module-size and search-result pages: remain noindex, follow.

## Delhi NCR location recommendation

Use the home/contact experience plus one potential Delhi NCR supplier page for location intent. Do not build city landing pages until the business can demonstrate actual service fulfilment and the page can carry unique local details (address/service coverage, fulfilment process, quotations, project examples or store information). A Google Business Profile and exact NAP consistency are higher-impact local foundations than city templates.
