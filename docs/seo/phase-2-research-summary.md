## 3. Top commercial keyword patterns discovered

- `[category] supplier/dealer/wholesaler Delhi` — strong for wires/cables, protection and switches.
- `Polycab/Finolex + wires + Delhi` — strongest brand × category local cluster, but initially owned by existing brand/category pages.
- `[brand] dealer Delhi` — commercial but must not use unsupported authorisation claims.
- `[category] price` — rejected as a page template; satisfy through accurate product/category content.
- `[brand] × [category] × [city]` — rejected as a scaled template.

## 4. Top organic competitors discovered

BRBD, Tegh Cables, Shree Jee Trading, M D Electricals Delhi, Best of Electricals, Justdial, and manufacturer-owned sites are the meaningful current SERP competitors. Details and positioning lessons are in `competitor-research.md`.

## 5. Highest-priority Delhi NCR opportunities

- A single strong Delhi NCR wires/cables category with bulk/project quote CTA.
- A single strong Delhi NCR circuit-protection category with rating/pole requirement CTA.
- A single Delhi NCR switches/sockets category with compatibility path into plates and boxes.
- Accurate business location, service area, hours and Google Business Profile/NAP consistency.
- One supplier page only after it has unique local fulfilment proof; no Noida/Gurugram/Ghaziabad/Faridabad templates now.

## 6. Pages we should NOT create

Do not create city × brand × category pages, thin MCB/RCCB price pages, brand/category duplicates, one page per colour/module/pack size, searchable/faceted result pages, or generic informational pages that have no purchasing bridge. Details and gates are in `commercial-page-map.md`.

## 7. Product indexation recommendation

Keep genuinely unique active product pages indexable; retain utility/facet pages as `noindex, follow`; resolve the 36 duplicate canonical routes; consolidate thin/near-duplicate variants; use factual canonicals/schema; and route discontinued products individually. Full policy: `product-indexation-strategy.md`.
