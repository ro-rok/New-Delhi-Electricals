## 1. Shree Anant Electric — strongest SEO competitor

Key SEMrush pages:
- `/electrical-wire.html` — **197 ranking keywords, ~2,381 traffic (77% of domain traffic)**
- `/` — 23 keywords, ~317 traffic
- `/finolex-wire.html` — 26 keywords, ~185 traffic
- `/optical-fiber-cable.html` — 21 keywords, ~117 traffic

Why it matters:
- A small number of **strong family/range pages**, not thousands of SKU pages, drive most traffic.
- Its dedicated Finolex wire page ranks #5 for `finolex wire dealer in delhi`.
- It ranks #3 for `electrical shops in delhi` in the keyword-gap snapshot.
- It ranks top 10 for many generic wire/price queries.

Weakness/opportunity:
- Authority Score is only 10; the advantage is architecture/relevance more than an unbeatable link moat.
- NDE can build cleaner dealer/range hubs with verified catalogue data and stronger local trust/WhatsApp conversion UX.

## 2. Entergy Electricals — cable-family specialist

Top pages:
- `/aluminium-armoured-cable.html` — 22 keywords, ~117 traffic
- `/polycab-aluminum-armoured-cable.html` — 7 keywords, ~24 traffic
- `/5-core-electric-power-cables.html` — 2 keywords, ~15 traffic

Lesson:
- Specific commercial product-family pages can rank even on a relatively small domain.
- Only copy this architecture where NDE actually carries the product family.

## 3. Ankit Switchgear

Top pages:
- `/hpl-wire.html` — ~15 traffic
- homepage — ~14
- `/ms-conduit-pipes.html` — ~10
- `/polycab-wire.html` — ~9
- `/electrical-cables.html` — ~5

Lesson:
- Brand×category pages are a valid search architecture, but not every page performs.
- NDE should create only combinations with measurable search demand and deep inventory.

## Large SERP competitors

### Manufacturers
- Havells
- Polycab
- Finolex
- Anchor/Panasonic
- Lauritz Knudsen

Manufacturer pages dominate broad brand/navigation queries and dealer locators. NDE should compete on:
- local dealer intent
- current range
- quotation/availability
- brand × category
- comparison/selection support

### Directories/marketplaces
- Justdial
- IndiaMART
- TradeIndia
- AuthorisedDealer.com

These frequently occupy local dealer SERPs. Their presence creates competition, but it also shows the SERP accepts local supplier pages rather than only manufacturer domains.

## SERP snapshots

### `finolex wire dealer in delhi`
Top positions include:
1. Finolex dealer locator
2. IndiaMART
3. Justdial
5. Shree Anant dedicated Finolex wire page
13–15. smaller local dealer/catalogue sites

**Interpretation:** realistic top-10 path for a strong local specialist page.

### `polycab wire dealer in delhi`
Local/specialist pages rank #2, #3, #7 and #10 alongside Polycab, directories and marketplaces.

**Interpretation:** one of the best new-page opportunities.

### `havells dealer in delhi`
Manufacturer store/partner pages dominate more heavily. A local supplier page appears around #5, but this SERP is harder than Finolex/Polycab.

**Interpretation:** optimize the existing Havells brand page rather than create a redundant “dealer Delhi” page.

### `electrical shops in delhi`
Mixed SERP: directories, a local electrical retailer, IndiaMART, Shree Anant, local-content publishers, YouTube and Sulekha.

**Interpretation:** relatively weak/mixed SERP where a strong homepage plus local entity signals can compete.

## Backlink gap

- NDE: 40 referring domains

This is meaningful but not a reason to delay commercial-page work. Shree Anant's traffic concentration shows page architecture/content relevance is a major part of its advantage.
