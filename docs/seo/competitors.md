# Competitor and SERP evidence

## Scope and limitation

Semrush Organic Competitors and Keyword Gap were blocked by zero connector API units. This is therefore a **live Google SERP competitor list**, not a ranked Semrush competitor report. Estimated traffic, keyword totals, overlap and backlink strengths are deliberately absent.

Observed searches on 2026-08-28 included `electrical dealer Delhi Havells Polycab Finolex Anchor Lauritz Knudsen`, `Havells authorised dealer Delhi switches MCB`, `Polycab wire dealer Delhi electrical shop`, and `MCB dealer Delhi electrical supplier`.

## Manufacturer and publisher SERP pressure

- Manufacturer-owned product/catalogue results can dominate exact brand/model queries. NDE should target the local purchase, availability and quotation layer it can genuinely fulfil rather than generic manufacturer education.
- Directories can dominate `near me`, dealer and vendor queries. A well-maintained Google Business Profile is necessary but is outside this repository.
