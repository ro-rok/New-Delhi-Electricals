## Quality filter (applied before anything reaches the list below)

A prospect qualifies only if all four hold:

1. A real business or organisation with its own audience, not a link inventory.
2. Topically adjacent — electrical supply, construction, building materials, interiors, trade, or
   Delhi local business.
3. The link would make sense to a reader who has never heard of SEO.
4. It is obtainable without buying an undisclosed ranking link.

**Rejected outright, no exceptions:** PBNs; guest-post farms and "write for us" networks selling
placements; automated link exchanges; mass directory blasts; global directories with no India or
electrical relevance; casino, crypto, adult or general-topic domains; sitewide footer links;
undisclosed paid dofollow links; sites whose outbound-link profile is visibly manipulated;
AI-generated content mills. Zero of these appear below.

## Linkable assets — 2 to 3 things worth citing

Nothing here invents data, runs a fake "study", or claims proprietary research.

### Asset 1 — House wiring material checklist for a Delhi NCR flat *(highest value, build first)*

A one-page, printable checklist covering every material a full domestic rewire needs: conductor
sizes by circuit type, insulation grade, coil lengths, MCB ratings and poles per circuit, RCCB
sensitivity, board module count, plates and mounting boxes — with a blank quantity column.

Why it earns links: it is the thing an owner, an architect and a contractor each need and nobody
publishes for the Indian domestic context. It is useful whether or not you buy from us.
Contractors will send it to clients; designers will attach it to specifications.

Build as an HTML page under `/guides/` with print styles, not a gated PDF. Gating it kills the
links. Ties to `/category/wires-cables`, `/category/circuit-protection`, `/category/plates`,
`/category/mounting-boxes` — four live commercial parents from one asset.

### Asset 2 — Modular switch and plate planning worksheet

A room-by-room planner: count the points, add the module widths, get the plate sizes. This is
exactly the arithmetic that goes wrong on a switch order, and it is the natural bridge into
`/category/switches-sockets`, `/brand/anchor/switches-sockets` and
`/brand/havells/switches-sockets` — the switches cluster that currently has no content because it
has no verified informational demand. A tool is not an article; it does not need search volume to
earn links from designers and contractors.

Depends on Cluster C research in week 3.

### Asset 3 — Electrical terminology reference for buyers

A single alphabetical reference for the terms that appear on Indian electrical quotations and
specifications: FR, FRLS, FR-LSH, HFFR, sq mm, 1100 V grade, IS 694, ISI/CM-L, MCB, MCCB, RCCB,
RCBO, ELCB, isolator, ACCL, trip curve, module width, gang, way, DP, SP, TP, FP. Plain
definitions, no marketing.

Why it earns links: reference pages get cited by people writing about something else. It is also
the natural internal hub for every guide, and it is cheap to maintain.

Lowest priority of the three — build after the checklist proves the format.

## Outreach frameworks

Personalisation is mandatory. Every message below names the specific page, the specific reason,
and a real person where one is identifiable. **No sequence is automated, and nothing is sent at
volume.** If a template can be sent unchanged to twenty prospects, it is not ready to send to one.

### A. Manufacturer dealer directory listing

> **Subject:** Authorised dealer listing — New Delhi Electricals, Malviya Nagar
>
> Hello [name],
>
> We are an authorised [brand] dealer at 30 A Corner Market, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi 110017,
> and we carry [specific range — e.g. the FR and FRLS house wire range from 0.75 to 35 sq mm].
>
> We are not currently on the dealer locator at [exact URL]. Could you add us? Our details are
> below. If the listing supports a website field, please use
> https://www.newdelhielectricals.com/brand/[brand] — it is the page showing the [brand] range
> we actually stock, which is more useful to a customer than our homepage.
>
> [NAP block]

### B. Unlinked mention / listing claim

> Hello,
>
> You have a listing for New Delhi Electricals at [exact URL]. It is our business, but it is
> missing our phone number and website, and the address should read 30 A Corner Market.
>
> Could you update it to: [NAP block], website https://www.newdelhielectricals.com/ — or tell me
> how to claim the listing and I will maintain it.

### C. Resource-page inclusion (contractors, designers, associations)

> Hello [name],
>
> Your [specific page title] lists suppliers for [specific thing]. I noticed it does not cover
> [specific gap — e.g. where to get a mixed-brand wiring schedule priced in one go].
>
> We are an authorised dealer for Lauritz Knudsen, Havells, Anchor, Polycab and Finolex in
> Malviya Nagar. Two things on our site may be worth linking rather than our homepage:
> [specific guide URL] and [specific category URL].
>
> No obligation either way — if it is not useful to your readers, please ignore this.

### D. Broken-link replacement

> Hello [name],
>
> The link to [dead URL] on [exact page URL] is returning [404 / redirecting to a parked domain].
>
> We published [specific guide URL] which covers the same ground — [one sentence on what it
> actually contains]. Use it if it fits; either way you may want to fix or remove the dead link.

### E. Expert contribution to a publication

> Hello [name],
>
> You cover [specific beat]. I run the counter at an authorised electrical dealership in South
> Delhi, and there are a few things we see every week that do not make it into published
> coverage — for instance, [one specific, concrete observation].
>
> If that is useful for a piece, I am happy to answer questions on the record. I am not looking
> to place an article; I am offering the perspective.

### F. Linkable-asset announcement

> Hello [name],
>
> We built [asset name] for our own counter customers: [one sentence on what it does]. It is a
> free web page, no email required, no download: [URL].
>
> Given [specific reason this prospect's audience needs it], it may be worth a link from
> [specific page]. If not, no reply needed.

**Never send:** anything offering payment for a link, anything implying reciprocity, anything sent
to a generic address at volume, or anything claiming a relationship that does not exist.

## Measurement

Track new referring domains weekly in `weekly-scorecard.md`. Count only domains that pass the
quality filter. **A month with three relevant manufacturer and local links is a better month than
one with forty directory submissions**, and the scorecard should not reward the second.
