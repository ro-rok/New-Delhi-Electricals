## What currently drives traffic

SEMrush attributes essentially **all estimated traffic (50/month)** to the homepage ranking #3 for `delhi electrical malviya nagar` (720 volume, KD 22). This query is navigational/local and is not a sensible strategic target to expand around.

The first clearly valuable commercial ranking is:

- `finolex dealers in delhi` — **position 29**, volume **110**, CPC **0.05**, KD **19** in Organic Research, URL `/brand/finolex`.

Keyword Analytics separately reports this phrase at KD **17**. The discrepancy is preserved rather than reconciled away.

Another important existing signal:

- `electrical company in delhi` — **position 46** on `/`, volume **320**, KD **23**, CPC **0.14`.
- The same query also ranks `/about` at **63** and `/brands` at **78**, which is a direct SEMrush cannibalization signal.
